Version: 1.0
Document Type: Reference Guide

Pre-Construction Verification

Before construction begins, the applicant should ensure that
property-related documents and approved building plans are available.
The field officer should review the submitted documents for completeness
and identify any missing information before the application proceeds.

Site Verification

A field officer may need to verify the property details and confirm
that the information provided in the application is consistent with
the available property documentation.

Construction Progress

Construction progress should be recorded at relevant stages of the
project. Photographs and field observations may be used to document
progress and identify potential issues.

Document Verification

If required property information or construction-related documentation
is unavailable, the field officer should request the missing information
from the applicant rather than assuming that the requirement has been
satisfied.
