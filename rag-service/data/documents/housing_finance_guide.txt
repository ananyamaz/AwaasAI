Housing Loan Documentation

A housing loan application may require identity proof, address proof,
income-related documents, bank statements, and property-related documents.
The exact documentation requirements depend on the applicant profile,
loan product, lender, and property.

Identity and Address Verification

Applicants may be asked to provide valid identity and address documents.
The documents should be clear and readable and the information should
match the details provided in the application.

Income Verification

Applicants may need to provide documents that demonstrate their income.
For salaried applicants, this may include salary slips, bank statements,
or other employment-related income evidence. For self-employed applicants,
income tax records and business-related financial documents may be relevant.

Property Documentation

Property-related documentation may include proof of ownership,
sale agreements, approved building plans, and other documents required
for property verification.

Document Verification Workflow

A field officer should first review the submitted documents for
completeness and readability. Missing or unclear documents should be
identified before the application proceeds to the next verification stage.

If required information is unavailable, the officer should request the
missing document from the applicant rather than assuming that the
requirement has been satisfied.
